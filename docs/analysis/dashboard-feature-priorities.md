## Purpose and basis

The group will design the Academic Performance Analytics Dashboard as one coherent application. The SRS and analysis models must describe the agreed features consistently. Diagrams should represent system concepts without visually prioritising particular features or labelling implementation phases. FEAT-24 remains subject to separate institutional decisions and is not built or populated with real mental-health data in the current prototype.

This scope draws on the assignment (DEC-001), 60 student questionnaire responses (STAKE-001), the [updated detailed faculty report](academic-dashboard-detailed-student-and-faculty-findings%20updated.docx), the [decision-sheet review](feature-decision-sheet-review-2026-10-23.md), the four-role working model and the four-system comparison. ## Agreed data-entry direction

**Marks:** After grading, the teacher prepares the Excel sheet already needed by the Controller of Examinations. A typical sheet has serial number, name, `faculty no.`, enrolment number, Sessional 1, Sessional 2 and total marks. The teacher uploads that same native Excel file to the dashboard; no second marks-entry grid is planned. Match students primarily by stable faculty/enrolment numbers, not serial number or name. Preview matched rows, reject missing/duplicate identifiers and invalid marks, and show a discrepancy if the sheet's total does not match the Teacher-selected sum/average rule. A corrected re-upload updates the intended records without duplication and retains history. The dashboard does not submit marks to the examination office or university portal. This is the reported typical layout, not a verified institution-wide workbook contract.

**Attendance:** For each subject/section and term, the assigned Teacher chooses either (a) an authorised Google Sheet, marked by date and read automatically by the dashboard, or (b) a dated-column Excel attendance workbook uploaded to the dashboard. Both use serial number, name, faculty number and enrolment number followed by session dates; a defined present/absent value is required and a blank remains unrecorded, not automatically absent. The same ID matching, validation and selected-month/cumulative calculations apply to either route. Sheet corrections are made in Sheets and resynced; workbook corrections are made in the file and re-uploaded. Display the active source, last successful sync **or** upload, and errors. Keep one active source per subject/section/term and reconcile a source switch by student ID and session date so attendance is not counted twice; retain source/version history. The dashboard does not write to the department website. Sheet access/sync, Excel template, codes, switching and excused-session rules still need confirmation (DEC-032/035/037/040).

Sessional 1 and 2 marks are shown separately, alongside one combined result calculated by sum or average as selected by the assigned Teacher for the subject (DEC-033). Students see only that selected combined result and its method, not a calculation switch. For a Master's student, a Sessional 1 or 2 mark below 15 out of 30 triggers a marks warning; an undergraduate receives no marks warning. Both groups still see their exact marks. Maximum marks, absent/missing values and rounding must be checked against the actual assessment rule before this warning is used beyond a demonstration.

Use de-identified examples of the marks workbook, attendance workbook and Sheet to test mapping, validation, correction, source switching, sync failure and safe replacement before freezing their data contracts. Google Sheets access should be limited to authorised files; the exact integration method must be tested before claiming automatic updates. [Google's Sheets API](https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/samples/reading) can read sheet values, and [its authorisation guidance](https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/scopes) requires an appropriate file-access scope.

## Use cases and role coverage for one build

1. **Admin creates accounts and programme/department access; HOD assigns Teachers to department teaching groups** (FEAT-01–03). Assigned Teachers manage attendance sources (FEAT-17).
2. **Teacher chooses Sheets or attendance Excel; dashboard syncs or imports and checks it** (FEAT-03–05, 11, 14, 17).
3. **Teacher uploads, reviews and corrects marks**, and selects the combined sessional method for an assigned subject (FEAT-03–05).
4. **Student views progress, shared calendar, notifications and AI guidance, and downloads a report** (FEAT-05–07, 10–11, 14, 22, 29–30).
5. **Teacher compares an assigned student's subjects and reviews class performance** (FEAT-08–09, 13).
6. **Teacher schedules a sessional, quiz or assignment deadline and creates a timed MCQ quiz; student attempts it** (FEAT-19, 29–30).
7. **Student completes and updates an academic background profile, uploads academic work and asks a Teacher a question** (FEAT-15, 18, 20).
8. **HOD reviews authorised department data and schedules a department event** (FEAT-09–12, 20 and 29 where authorised).

The use-case set should consider student-support questions where relevant without giving them a dedicated diagram solely because of implementation timing. Do not invent a mandatory response or Teacher access policy. Detail only 5–8 major use cases as required by ADIS; unresolved cases may remain at a high level until proper requirements are gathered.

The four current design roles are **Student, Teacher, HOD and Admin**. Admin is a technical and setup role; editing or releasing academic records needs explicit permission. Teachers can read released academic results and self-reported background/interests for students in their assigned teaching groups, but change only their own subject records; students edit their own background profile. The proposed model gives Teachers release/correction rights for their subject and HOD department-limited academic visibility; exact operations and fields still need a permission matrix and staff review. A qualified support owner is not added as an actor without an agreed workflow. The Controller of Examinations and department attendance staff remain external stakeholders unless a confirmed workflow gives them dashboard accounts.

The screen-design subjects now include login and first-sign-in academic profile; Admin setup; attendance-source choice, Sheet link/sync status and attendance Excel upload/validation; separate marks Excel upload/validation; Student overview/detail/report; Teacher class, cross-subject and student-background view; attendance warnings; timed MCQ creation/attempts; questions/feedback; academic-work upload; calendar creation/view; notification centre; AI guidance; HOD department views; and any student-support concept justified by elicitation. Group the subjects into the ADIS-required 5–8 important wireframes after scope review. The eight candidate use cases above need main, alternate and exception flows before their UML diagram is frozen.

## Decisions recorded and checks before SRS and diagram freeze

The group selected the following design inputs on 24 October 2026. They are project choices or reported typical formats, not confirmation of university policy:

1. **Marks workbook:** Serial number, name, `faculty no.`, enrolment number, Sessional 1, Sessional 2 and total marks are the expected columns. Inspect a de-identified example for exact headers, maximums, absent codes and layout variants before freezing import rules.
2. **Attendance source choice:** Both the Google Sheet and attendance Excel workbook use serial number, name, faculty number and enrolment number before date columns. Define present/absent/blank/excused values, Sheet ownership and one-way sync cadence, Excel upload validation, correction/version behaviour and safe source switching with realistic examples. Only one source is active per subject/section/term.
3. **Permissions:** Retain the proposed Teacher own-subject edit/release/correction and cross-subject read model, HOD department academic access and Admin setup role. “Go with everything as mentioned” does not specify every field or reopening operation; record the exact matrix and obtain the required staff review.
4. **Marks and subject review:** Retain separate Sessional 1/2 and the Teacher-selected sum or average. Master's sessionals use a below-15-out-of-30 warning; undergraduate marks have no warning. Show class averages in both levels, with the below-cutoff count where a cutoff applies. Check score maxima, rounding, missing/absent handling and comparison population.
5. **Attendance, quizzes and academic work:** The attendance warning is student-only and appears below 30%; percentages and counts remain visible without a warning at or above 30%. Teachers set MCQ questions, count and timer. Students can upload/update academic work. Define timer expiry, attempts, file limits, verification, reports and message handling in the SRS.
6. **Cost and wellbeing proposal:** The entire implementation must require no payment. Evaluate local Ollama or currently free hosted models for academic guidance without enabling billing or transferring unapproved identifiable data. Analyse the requested FEAT-24 questionnaire, AI support signal and Teacher profile view, but keep mandatory participation, automatic visibility, questions, access/retention, human review and referral unvalidated. No FEAT-24 implementation, real responses or AI health labels belong in the current prototype.
7. **Testing and pre-implementation review:** Faculty usability testing of the implemented workflows can occur after coding. The ADIS handout separately requires the problem definition, requirements and major analysis models to be reviewed and approved **before** implementation; testing later cannot replace that review.

8. **Student background:** Treat first sign-in as profile setup rather than asking for the same details at every login. Confirm the exact school/degree fields, which entries may be left blank, and whether student updates require a visible history. Teachers' access stays read-only and limited to their assigned students. This profile is not an input to health judgement or an official academic score.

The new calendar and notifications need subject/department audience rules, event changes or cancellation behaviour, reminder timing and duplicate prevention. They are in scope as in-app features so a no-cost implementation is plausible, but the hosting and integration plan still needs TELOS review. [Google Sheets standard API use currently has no additional charge within its quotas](https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/limits); free model availability and limits can change, so the project must not rely on paid spillover. [OpenRouter's free tier](https://openrouter.ai/pricing) is rate-limited and may not be suitable for confidential student data; local inference needs a hardware check.

Use the combined [PS-002/REQ-003 draft](dashboard-problem-and-requirements.md) to validate the remaining decisions, stakeholder permissions, ADIS elicitation report and TELOS study before agreeing the feature baseline. Student background, calendar, notifications, both attendance-source choices, cross-subject view, quiz, academic-work, feedback, academic AI and the analysed support concept should be covered across the appropriate models without crowding any one diagram. Track implementation boundaries in the SRS and implementation plan, outside the drawings. Freeze the diagrams only after they are consistent with the approved requirement baseline.
