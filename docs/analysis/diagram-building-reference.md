## Drawing rules

**Coverage and readability.** The inventory has 21 diagrams. CTX-01 and DFD-00 give breadth; detailed views show the decisions, messages or states named in their rows. Use standard symbols, legible labels and whitespace. If a view becomes crowded, simplify its level of detail before making another feature-specific page. [UCSPEC-001](../diagrams/use-case-specifications.md) holds nine earlier scenarios. Use SC-01/02 and the profile/work part of SC-06 for UC-01; SC-03/04 and the HOD review part of SC-07 for UC-02; SC-05 and the question part of SC-06 for UC-03; the scheduling/notice part of SC-07 and conceptual SC-09 for UC-04; and SC-08 for UC-05. Rewrite SC-08 for the agreed Admin scope before drawing UC-05. HOD Teacher assignment is confirmed for UC-01; broader academic setup authority still needs confirmation.

**Notation.** Each use-case page names and draws all actors outside the system boundary, with separate associations to the cases they actually initiate or participate in. `<<include>>` points from a base case to behaviour that always occurs; `<<extend>>` points from conditional behaviour to its base case and labels the condition. For UC-05, label the manual/CSV conditions and make their choice exclusive. Do not add these links just to connect ovals. DFD arrows name data and external entities never connect directly to stores. Number Level 1 processes as 2.x and 3.x and balance their external flows with DFD-00. Each activity page draws the named swimlanes, places actions in their responsible lane and uses guards at decisions; a branch involving a second person must show the handoff. Sequence diagrams use ordered messages and `alt`/`opt` frames. State diagrams follow one named object, not screen navigation.

**Data and access.** Blank, missing, zero, absent, unreleased and stale are different states. Sessional 1/2 and the Teacher-selected sum or average remain distinct from quiz scores. An assigned Teacher edits only their subject and reads other released subject results for their assigned Students; HOD access is department-scoped and includes Teacher-to-class assignment. Admin account rights do not imply academic editing; programme/subject setup authority remains TBD. One attendance source is active per subject/section/term, and switching it must not double-count a session.

**ER and implementation boundary.** ER-01 is the agreed **conceptual core**, not proof that ten physical tables store every selected feature. Keep Student, Teacher, HOD and Admin separate; do not draw `UserAccount`, `Offering` or `TeachingEnrollment` as ER entities. Show Department offers Programme, Student enrols in Programme, Programme contains Subject, Teacher selects Attendance Source, and Marks/Attendance Record relate to the relevant Teacher-Subject-Student combination. Resolve the ternary relationship's SQL mapping and storage for quiz, work, feedback, calendar and notices during logical database design. FEAT-24 has no approved physical storage or real-response workflow.

**Rules still needing confirmation.** Programme/subject setup authority, mark release/reopening authority, attendance codes and source-switch behaviour, HOD field access, quiz attempt policy, work verification, feedback escalation and several ER cardinalities are not institutionally confirmed. Mark the affected guard or relation **TBD** rather than completing the diagram with an invented rule. A report is generated from released records; do not draw a stored report lifecycle unless the SRS later requires one.

## Files and review

Keep editable sources and review exports in [docs/diagrams](../diagrams/README.md). Draw.io (`.drawio`) is the default; Visual Paradigm (`.vpp`), PlantUML (`.puml`) or Mermaid (`.mmd`) are options when the notation remains clear. Export SVG and, when useful, PNG. Keep one editable master per ID and label each drawing with its ID, title, version and review status. Do not edit exports separately.

The existing UC-01 to UC-05 and marks-only ACT-01 are **prior-plan drafts**, not drawings that satisfy this inventory. Retain them for comparison while revising UC-01 to UC-05 and ACT-01. Check role scope, DFD balance, requirement traceability, readable exports and the ADIS review gate before freezing any drawing. Derive the logical database schema after ER review and record the gap between the ten-entity core and full feature scope.
